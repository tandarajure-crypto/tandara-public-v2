FIRST BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

1.0. Jure Tandara – son of Ivan (born c. 1725)

1.0. Jure Tandara, born between 1720 and 1725, was the eldest son of Ivan Tandarić, who later established Tandara as the permanent family surname. Jure married Matija Lekić. They had one son, 1.1. Frane, and two daughters, Anđa and Ana.

Family member codes: 0.0 Ivan Tandara ; 1.0-SP-1 Matija Lekić ; 1.1 Frane Jurin ; 1.0-K1 Anđa Jurina ; 1.0-K2 Ana Jurina .

1.1. Frane Jurin

1.1. Frane Jurin was married to Boža Ćosić. Their son Nikola was born in 1792.

Family member codes: 1.1-SP-1 Boža Ćosić ; 1.1.1 Nikola Franin .

1.1.1. Nikola Franin (1792–)

1.1.1. Nikola Franin was the only child of Frane and Boža Ćosić. It is not known whether he married or had children.

The 1806 census of Ričice records no member of Jure's family. What happened to this family line is unknown. It is assumed that they moved away, after which all trace of them was lost.

It would be interesting if a descendant of this branch were to make contact one day and confirm their connection to Jure's family line.
