THE FIRST BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

1.0. Jure Tandara — son of Ivan (★ c. 1725 – ✝ N.G.)

Jure Tandara, born between 1720 and 1725, was the eldest son of Ivan Tandarić, who later established the family surname as Tandara. Jure married Matija Lekić, and they had a son, Frane, and two daughters, Anđa and Ana.

Family member codes: 0.0 Ivan Tandara (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) ; 1.0-SP-1 Matija Lekić (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) ; 1.1 Frane (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) ; 1.0-K1 Anđa (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) ; 1.0-K2 Ana (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) .

1.1. Frane — son of Jure (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.)

Frane, son of Jure, was married to Boža Ćosić. In 1792 they had a son, Nikola.

Family member codes: 1.1-SP-1 Boža Ćosić (★ N.G. – ✝ N.G.) ; 1.1-S1 Nikola (★ 1792 – ✝ N.G.) .

1.1-S1. Nikola — son of Frane (★ 1792 – ✝ N.G.)

Nikola was the only child of Frane and Boža Ćosić. It is not known whether he married or had children.

In the 1806 census of Ričice, no members of Jure's family were recorded. What happened to this family line remains unknown. It is assumed that they moved away, after which all trace of them was lost.

It would be of great interest if one of the descendants of this branch were to come forward one day and confirm their connection to Jure's family line.
